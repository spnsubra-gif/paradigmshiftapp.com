What Lunga is

Lunga is an app for booking local services where you are staying in Bali: laundry, scooter rental, massages and more. The services are provided by independent local businesses (the vendors). Lunga takes your booking and your payment, passes the booking to the vendor, and helps if anything goes wrong.

Your account

You log in with your phone number, Apple or Google. Please give your real name and a phone number where we can reach you, and keep access to them to yourself. You can delete your account at any time from the account page.

Prices and paying

Prices are in Indonesian rupiah. Before you pay you see the price of the service, the service fee and the total. You pay in the app by card, Apple Pay or Google Pay; payments are handled by Stripe.

Cancelling

Each booking shows how long you can cancel it for free. Cancel in time and you get everything back, including the service fee. After that, we refund only if the vendor doesn’t turn up or something goes wrong: message us and we check with the vendor.

The vendors

Vendors set their own prices and are responsible for their service. Anything paid directly to a vendor, such as a scooter deposit, is between you and the vendor.

Contact
