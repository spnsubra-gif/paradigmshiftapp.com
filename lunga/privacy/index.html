Privacy Policy

Last updated 4 November 2026

What we keep

Your name, your phone number, the email Apple or Google shares when you log in with them, your profile picture if you add one, the language you chose, where you are staying (the pin on the map, its address, the place's name, your room, unit or floor, and your note on how to find you), and your bookings. Card details go straight to Stripe; Lunga never sees your card number.

Why

To log you in (we send your code by WhatsApp), to take your bookings and payments, to send receipts, and to reach you on the day if anything changes.

Who sees what

The vendor you book gets your name, where you are staying (the place, your room, unit or floor, and the exact pin) and your notes, never your phone number. Stripe handles payments. Our data is stored with Supabase in Singapore. Codes are sent by WhatsApp (Meta). The map and the address search use Google Maps.

How long

Your account stays until you delete it. Bookings are kept for payouts, refunds and tax, also after an account is deleted, without the link to the account.

Deleting your account

On the account page, tap Delete account. Your sign-in and your profile are deleted straight away.

Contact

Message Lunga on WhatsApp from the Support tab in the app.

You can also email lunga@paradigmshiftapp.com or visit Lunga support.

Who runs Lunga
