How it works

  1. Pick what you need

    A scooter, a laundry pickup or a massage, with prices shown upfront in rupiah.

  2. Pay by card

    Choose a time and pay in the app. No cash, no haggling, no waiting for replies.

  3. It comes to you

    Your scooter is delivered, your laundry is picked up, or your therapist comes to your villa.

What you can book

Scooters

Daily, weekly or monthly rentals, delivered to where you're staying.

Laundry

Picked up and brought back, priced per kilo.

Massage

Run by local businesses

Every booking goes to a local rental shop, laundry or massage therapist around Canggu. They set their own prices and get paid in full.
