How do refunds work?

Cancel within the free window shown on your booking and you get everything back, including the service fee. After that, we refund only if the vendor doesn't turn up or something goes wrong: message us and we check with the vendor.

Can I pay in cash?

Bookings are paid in the app by card, so there's no cash to sort out with the vendor. Prices are in rupiah, paid in the app.

Who are the vendors?

Local businesses near where you are staying, and each one sets its own price.

Deleting your account

In the app, open Account, then Manage account, and tap Delete account. Your sign-in and your profile are deleted straight away. Can't open the app? Message us on WhatsApp or email us from the phone number or email on your account and we delete it for you. Bookings are kept for payouts, refunds and tax, without the link to your account.
