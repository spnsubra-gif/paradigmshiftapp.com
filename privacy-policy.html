Privacy Policy

Last updated: [insert date before publishing]

Luna ("Luna," "the App," "we," "us," "our") is developed and operated by Paradigm Shift Apps, based in Singapore. Luna is currently our first app, with more planned under the Paradigm Shift Apps name — where relevant, this Policy is written to apply across our current and future products.

By downloading or using Luna, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

1. Information we collect

Account information. Your email address and authentication credentials, handled through our backend provider, Supabase. Passwords are hashed and stored securely by Supabase; we never see them in plain text.

Sleep and wellness data. Bedtime and wake times, alarm settings, sleep logs, and any task, log, or journal-style entries you create in the App, along with the AI-generated check-ins and weekly reflections the App produces for you.

Alarm verification photos. If you turn on "Require a photo to turn off alarm," the photo you take is sent to Anthropic's Claude AI vision API for a brief, lenient check that it was taken in daylight or adequate brightness.

To confirm: we intend not to retain these photos any longer than needed to complete verification, and they are not used for facial recognition or any purpose beyond dismissing the alarm — confirm this matches actual behavior before publishing.

Usage and device data. General app usage information used to maintain and troubleshoot the service.

What we don't collect. Luna does not access your precise location, contacts, or microphone.

2. How we use your information

  • To provide core functionality: sleep tracking, alarms, tasks, and Unwind sounds
  • To generate AI-powered check-ins and weekly reflections, using Anthropic's Claude API
  • To authenticate your account and support password reset, via Supabase
  • To maintain, secure, and improve the App

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your data for third-party advertising.

3. Third-party service providers

Luna relies on the following processors to operate:

  • Supabase — authentication, database, and secure storage of your account and app data
  • Anthropic (Claude API) — processes the text of your check-ins/reflections and your alarm-verification photos to generate AI content, under Anthropic's own data-handling terms for API customers

These providers may process or store data on servers located outside your home country. Because Luna is developed in Singapore and used primarily by people in the United States, your information may be transferred to and processed in Singapore, the United States, or other countries where our providers operate.

4. Data retention

We retain your account and sleep data for as long as your account stays active, or as needed to provide the service. You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time — see Section 6.

5. Data security

  • Session tokens are stored using device-level secure storage, not plain local storage
  • Data in transit is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS)
  • Our backend functions enforce rate limiting, POST-only request handling, and schema validation

See our Security page for further detail.

6. Your rights and choices

You can access, correct, or request deletion of your data at any time by emailing suria@paradigmshiftapp.com.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights. For example, California residents have rights under the CCPA/CPRA to know, delete, and correct their personal information, and to opt out of "sale or sharing" of data (we do not sell or share your data with third parties for advertising).

Needs legal review: some US states give extra protection to "consumer health data," which sleep-tracking information may fall under. This section should be expanded with a lawyer before launch, particularly if you expect meaningful numbers of users in Washington, Nevada, or Connecticut.

7. Children's privacy

Luna is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

8. International users

Luna is developed in Singapore and made available to users in the United States and elsewhere. By using Luna, you consent to your information being processed and stored as described in this Policy, which may take place outside your country of residence.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We'll update the "Last updated" date above, and where the change is material, we'll provide additional notice in the App.
